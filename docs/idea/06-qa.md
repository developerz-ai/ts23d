# 06 — QA gates

**A gate finding is a build failure, not a warning.** `t3d build` runs every default gate on every LOD and exits 1 on any finding. The agent iterates until it prints `QA PASS`. Source: `src/qa/`.

## Shipped (as of 2026-10)

`DEFAULT_GATES`, run per LOD by `runQa(compiled)`:

| Gate | Runs at | Fails when | Fix line it prints |
|---|---|---|---|
| `triangle-budget` | Every LOD | Total triangles at LOD *n* > `budget.triangles[n]` | Drop detail at this LOD (read `ctx.lod`) or raise the budget with a reason |
| `floating-parts` | Every LOD | A part's **surface** doesn't come within 2 cm of the ground (z ≈ 0) or of a grounded part's surface, transitively | Move it onto what holds it (check `at`, parent, LOD-gated detail around it). Bounding boxes touching is not enough |
| `gaps` | LOD0 | A slit 6 mm – 5 cm wide, ≥ 10 cm long, between two parts' surfaces, drops ≥ 10 cm behind both rims (or is see-through) seen from ±X, ±Y or +Z: a door short of its header, a panel that misses its frame, a floor plate short of the rails. Message: width, length, depth, which parts, `(x, y, z)` and side | Close it (extend/move one part until they meet). A real opening (vent, door seam, running clearance) is declared: `gaps: [{ parts: ['track_*'], why: '…' }]`. See it: `t3d gaps` |
| `clashes` | Every LOD | Another part penetrates more than 1 cm into the volume a moving part sweeps through its whole motion | Move or trim either part for running clearance; if they interlock by design, `clearOf: ['<part>']` on the moving part |
| `connections` | LOD0 | A shell inside a part doesn't reach the body over a real contact face (islands, point/edge contacts); a grown appendage (horn, spine, ear, wing, tail, tooth…) shows its root ring or a hard crease; a part pokes out the far side; an eye is exposed, stares or diverges; an unblended seam inside a sculpt | Seat or merge the piece; sink and blend the root (boss/fillet); declare intended joins `connect: { as: 'hinge'|'free'|'rest'…, why }` or folds in `creases`. See it: `connectionsOverlay` |
| `part-names` | Every LOD | A part name isn't `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*` | Rename; engines bind to node names |
| `proportions` | LOD0, if `real` is set | Overall world span along X / Y / Z is off `real.length` / `width` / `height` by more than `real.tolerance` (default ±5%) | Rescale along that axis; re-derive lengths from the `sources`, don't fudge the tolerance |
| `check` | Every LOD, if `checks` is set | A recipe-declared check returns a finding (`clear`, `noOverlap`, `touches`, `symmetric`, or your own) | The check's own `fix` |
| `measures` | LOD0, if `real.measures` is set | A spec-sheet dimension (driver diameter, gauge, wheelbase, turret width) measured on the model is off by more than its `tolerance` (default ±2%), or names no part | Rebuild the part from the spec number. **Never edit the spec to match the model** |

Each finding: `{ gate, part?, message, fix }`. Text output:

```
tiger_1  QA FAIL  tris 3108/1660/724
  ✗ floating-parts [exhaust_0]: LOD0: 'exhaust_0' touches nothing connected to the ground
    fix: move it onto what holds it (check `at`, parent and the LOD-gated detail around it) — …
```

### How each geometric gate measures

| Gate | Method | Tolerance constant |
|---|---|---|
| `floating-parts` | AABB prefilter, then surface samples vs triangles of the other part (point ↔ triangle distance, `TriangleGrid` spatial index), grown from the ground outward | `CONTACT_EPS` = 0.02 m |
| `clashes` | Per motion root (a spinning axle group, a rotor, a sliding crosshead) and its subtree: **spin** → exact solid of revolution (radius profile per axial slice, 24 slices); **sway** → the same full revolution, conservatively; **slide** → the moving mesh's bounds extended along the stroke. Every other part's surface samples are tested against that volume | `CLASH_EPS` = 0.01 m |
| `gaps` | Axis-aligned orthographic depth views (z-buffer with a part id per pixel, 1/1600 of the longest side, ≥ 3 mm), scanned along rows and columns for narrow trenches whose two rims are solid surface for ≥ 8 cm and level within 8 cm of each other (a crack between neighbours, not a rod in front of a wall). Skipped: the underside (−Z), slits inside one part (authored repeats: track links, crystals; `withinParts: true` to include), rims on a spinning subtree (running clearance) or a non-opaque surface, and declared `gaps` | width 6 mm – 5 cm, depth ≥ 10 cm, length ≥ 10 cm |
| `proportions` | Union of world-space part bounds at LOD0 | `real.tolerance`, default 0.05 |
| `measures` | Extent of the named parts along `axis` (prefix match, so `axle_driver_0` includes its wheels), or the centre-to-centre distance of two parts (`between`) | per measure, default 0.02 |

Order in `DEFAULT_GATES`: `triangleBudget`, `floatingParts`, `gaps`, `clashes`, `partNames`, `proportions`, `measures`, `recipeChecks` (gate name `check`).

### Recipe checks

Static intersections need declared intent: a bolt sunk into a plate is fine, a track through the hull is not. `checks: [...]` on `defineAsset` states which is which ([02-dsl](02-dsl.md#checks-declared-intent)). `clear` decides "inside" by ray parity against the other part's closed solid, so an open planar sheet never counts as a solid. Penetration over 1 cm fails.

Seeing and testing gaps: `t3d gaps <recipe>` writes `<out>/<asset>/gaps/overlay.png` (six axis views, undeclared slits red and ringed, declared ones blue), `zoom_<i>.png` per slit and `gaps.json`. In recipe tests (`@developerz.ai/ts23d/testing`): `assertNoGaps(asset)`, `assertTouches(asset, 'door_*', 'frame')` (surfaces within 2 mm, not the 2 cm that keeps a part attached), `assertFlush(asset, 'door_l', 'frame', { view: '+X' })` (faces level within 3 mm where they meet). From code: `scanGaps`, `flushness`, `gapOverlay`, `gapZoom`.

Helpers exported for custom gates: `touching`, `pointTriangleDistance`, `surfaceSamples`, `TriangleGrid`, `measure`, `insideSolid`, `glob`, `inspect`.

### Declaring what QA checks

```ts
export const loco = defineAsset({
  name: 'loco_440',
  kind: 'vehicle',
  sources: ['https://example.org/roster-sheet.pdf', 'Builder drawing no. 1234'],
  real: {
    length: 15.6, width: 2.9, height: 4.4,                       // proportions: ±5%
    measures: [                                                  // measures: ±2% each
      { label: 'driver diameter', value: 1.52, axis: 'z', parts: ['axle_driver_0'], source: 'roster' },
      { label: 'gauge', value: 1.435, axis: 'y', between: ['rail_l', 'rail_r'] },
      { label: 'driving wheelbase', value: 2.44, axis: 'x', between: ['axle_driver_0', 'axle_driver_1'] },
    ],
  },
  budget: { triangles: [20000, 8000, 2000] },
  build(ctx) {
    // a crosshead slides in its guide by design:
    // ctx.part('crosshead_l', mesh, { motion: { kind: 'slide', offset: [0.3, 0, 0], hz: 1 }, clearOf: ['guide_l'] });
  },
});
```

The rule for agents: **a failing `measures` finding means the model is wrong.** Rebuild the part from the spec; editing `value` or widening `tolerance` to pass is a defect.

### Known limits

- Point-in-solid (`clear`, `clashes` depth) needs **closed, non-overlapping** parts. Split structure from dressing: a closed hull part, with bolts and straps as separate parts, not one self-intersecting soup.
- `clashes` sweeps `sway` through its sector: 5° bins, each bin's occupied angles tested exactly at the sector edges (a leaf hung 3 mm off its post swings clear).
- `gaps` looks along the axes only: a slit seen only obliquely is missed, and sub-pixel seams (< 6 mm) are deliberately ignored. Check the contact sheet too.

- `proportions` measures overall bounds, so a stray part (a too-long barrel) can fail it, or mask a wrong hull. Check the header dims on the contact sheet.
- `clashes` uses bounds for slides, so an angled slide's volume is conservative; declare `clearOf` for a guide it rides in.
- `floating-parts` has no opt-out for things that genuinely fly (an aircraft in flight, a hanging sign). Planned, M1.
- Custom gates: `runQa(asset, [...DEFAULT_GATES, myGate])` from code. The CLI runs the defaults only.

## Adding a gate

1. A `Gate` is `(asset: CompiledAsset) => Finding[]`. Pure; world-space meshes via `worldMeshes` / `worldTransforms`.
2. Every finding carries a `fix` an agent can act on without reading the gate's source.
3. One file in `src/qa/`, a test next to it with one passing and one failing asset.
4. Add it to `DEFAULT_GATES` only when every recipe in `recipes/` passes it.

## Critique: a scored, attested look before an asset counts as done

QA gates prove an asset is *valid*; nothing in them proves it is *good*. The critique closes that
gap without a human polish pass: a vision-capable agent scores the renders against a fixed rubric,
and the score is committed next to the images it judged.

**The pack** — `t3d critique <recipe> --out <dir>` writes `<dir>/<asset>/critique/`:

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `hero.png` · `sheet.png` | Byte-identical to the committed `out/<asset>/` images (the pack warns `critique-drift` when they differ: run `bun run shots <name>` first) |
| `raking.png` · `raking_detail.png` | Camera at −115°, 8° up, so the studio key (50° camera-left, 55° up) grazes the near −Y side at ~81° incidence: relief reads as shadow, painted-on detail stays flat |
| `lods.png` | LOD0 beside LOD2, one camera |
| `refs.md` | Every link in the recipe's README dossier, photos first — links only, photos are never downloaded into the repo |
| `rubric.md` | The fixed rubric, the QA summary (findings, triangles per LOD) and the output contract |
| `critique.template.json` | The shape to fill, with the sha256 of every image prefilled |

**The rubric** (`RUBRIC` in `src/qa/critique-rubric.ts`), each 0–10 where 8 = ships in a commercial
game as-is: `silhouette`, `proportions` (vs the dossier), `materials` (believability), `detail`
(LOD0 density), `wear` (story, imperfection), `lod` (consistency LOD0 → LOD2), `technical` (QA
summary, budget). Every score below 10 names concrete recipe fixes.

**The attestation** — `recipes/<name>/out/<asset>/critique.json`:
`{ asset, reviewer: 'agent' | 'human', overall, scores: { <criterion>: { score, reason, fixes[] } }, renders: { 'hero.png': sha256, 'sheet.png': sha256, … } }`.
The recipes gate (`scripts/lib/critique.ts`) reports an asset when its critique is missing,
malformed, **stale** (the committed `hero.png` / `sheet.png` no longer hash to what was judged — the
asset changed, judge again) or any criterion scores below `CRITIQUE_MIN_SCORE` (8, repo-wide, no
per-recipe overrides). `bun run shots` carries `critique.json` across a refresh, so a changed render
shows up as stale rather than as missing.

**Phase-in**: `CRITIQUE_MODE = 'warn'` prints the findings and keeps the gate green while the
existing assets get their first critiques; flipping it to `'enforce'` makes them fail the build.
First critiques (warn mode, honest): boxcar 6.7, cargo_container_scifi 5.9, tiger_1 6.0 overall —
wear and LOD consistency are the weakest criteria across all three.

**Planned**: a zebra / reflection-line render for smooth surfaces (needs a per-pixel normal pass
from `/render`); a `light` override on beauty renders so raking light can come from any side and
elevation (today it is derived from the fixed studio rig); an optional CI fresh-eyes reviewer that
re-scores each PR's packs.

## Connections: stuff is nicely connected (gate `connections`, shipped)

`floating-parts` asks "within 2 cm of something grounded?" — a part can pass and still look glued on,
hang on a corner, or hide loose islands inside a merged mesh. `connections` (default gate, LOD0)
judges how pieces join (`src/qa/connections*.ts`, codes in [errors](../architecture/errors.md)):

| Check | Method | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Every piece reaches the ground or main body | Nodes = every **shell** of every part (welded at 0.1 mm). Edges: surface samples of the smaller piece vs a BVH of the other (distance) and its solid index (embedded). Strong = embedded or a tight-tolerance contact patch ≥ 10 % of the piece's section² (section from 4·V/A). A cluster held only by kisses still counts when its contacts add up to that, or span it (two contacts ≥ half its size apart: a slat across a vent, a rung between stiles) | `T3D_CONNECT_ISLAND`, `T3D_CONNECT_POINT` |
| `grown` roots (creature/character appendages, or declared) | Long axis of the piece out of its parent; 16 sectors around it — each sector's deepest point must be ≥ δ inside the parent; outside the root, the median angle between the piece's and the parent's normals (≤ 55° = blended); exposure on both sides of the buried band = poke-through; > 5 % inside an unrelated part = poke into it | `_GLUED` (ring visible %), `_CREASE` (blend radius), `_POKE`, `_BURIED` (warning) |
| Eyes (`eye`, `eyeball`, `eye_l` … on organic kinds = `socket`) | Pupil = darkest colour (gaze = centre → pupil); enclosed = inside the head or covered along the normal within an eye width; visible sclera above the iris top from the gaze direction; left/right gaze mirror-symmetric (characters: not diverging) | `_EYE_EXPOSED`, `_EYE_STARE`, `_EYE_GAZE` |
| Seams inside sculpts (organic kinds, ≥ 1500-triangle shells) | Concave, smooth-shaded folds (split normals = an authored hard edge, ignored) whose radius of curvature is < 1.5 % of the mesh size; chained along shared vertices, ≥ 4 % of the size long; folds hidden inside other solids and next to open borders skipped | `_SEAM` (location, length, fold angle, blend radius) |

Declare intent per part: `connect: { as: 'grown' | 'welded' | 'bolted' | 'rest' | 'hinge' | 'socket' |
'free', to?, why? }` (`free` needs `why`; `ctx.use` prefixes `to`). Defaults: `grown` for appendage name
segments and `socket` for eyes on `character` / `creature`; `rest` for running gear (wheel, track,
roller, idler, sprocket, axle, link, bogie) and `surface: 'cutout'` cards; `welded` otherwise.
Intended sculpt creases: `defineAsset({ creases: [{ name: 'nasolabial', part: 'head', at, radius, why }] })`.

See it: `connectionsOverlay(asset, { views?, parts?, focus? })` — red = error, orange dots = seam
line, yellow = warning, green = a grown/socket joint that passed. Tests: `assertConnected(asset)`,
`assertGrown(asset, 'horn_*')` (`/testing`). Cost: 0.1–3 s per asset, ~11 s for the 875k-triangle
roadster and the 15k-member Eiffel tower.

Known limits: a cluster spanning two kisses passes (two corner contacts read as a span);
`CREASE` needs a closed parent; seams are judged per shell, not across two parts (that is `CREASE`).
