# ts23d 0.20.0

Released 2026-11-21 · [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@developerz.ai/ts23d/v/0.20.0) · [diff v0.19.0...v0.20.0](https://github.com/developerz-ai/ts23d/compare/v0.19.0...v0.20.0) · [all releases](README.md) · [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md)

Renders stop borrowing colour from neighbouring texture rows, busts get carved faces, real ears, sewn clothes and far-LOD hair shells, and the RTS faction gets rain-stained walls, recessed windows, worn roofs and a lighter scene.

### Changed
- Human, elf and goblin busts have the bones of a face: a brow shelf over a carved orbit, lids with a margin of real thickness round an almond aperture, a lateral orbital rim where the face turns to its side, cheekbones over a buccal plane, each from a per-race `Structure` table (`recipes/_lib/bust/face-forms.ts`) and each measured on the built skin (`face-check.ts`; D570, D571, D572). The elf's and the goblin's ears are leaves of 4–5.5 mm cartilage with a rolled helix, antihelix and concha (`ear-graft.ts`): the elf's tip ends between brow and crown, clear of the hair; the goblin's sweep out and back (D573, D574). The human's neck is a man's 120 mm sloping into the shoulders (D575); the goblin's teeth stand in a mouth between lips (D576).
- The Saltmarch faction scene (rts_faction) is lighter and its wall reads as a wall: Warden and Delver bodies draw their small rounded forms at LOD1 rings (D590), the tank and the Warden Hall drop triangles the strategy camera never sees (scene LOD0 148k → 131k, tank 37.5k → 30.5k, hall 36.5k → 34.1k, D596), and the perimeter rises to 1.2 m on a plinth course darkened by brine grime (D592).
- Warden Hall (rts_barracks): rain stains under the sills are soft vertex-colour runs on the stones instead of grey icicle decals (D591); weathered slates cluster in wind patches at the eaves, ridge and verges, lost ones showing sarking and battens (D593); windows sit 18 cm back in stone reveals with timber casements and a dimmer amber light (D594).
- The Brinehound tank's bare steel (bolts, rails, sprockets, gun sleeve) is a polished gunmetal that catches the light, and its enamel has a satin sheen (D595).
- Wardens and Delvers stand in an easy A-stance and each instance turns its head its own way, from its seed (D597).

### Fixed
- water_tower: the goose-neck spout is sheet iron, not a bark-covered log (its tubes named no strip, which the old row bleed blurred).
- goblin_head: the head/bust part split no longer saws the outer halves of the ears off (D577).
- Human, elf and goblin busts' clothes: no grey patches down the sleeves or staircase lines (each garment triangle on its own projection plane at true scale, D578), the arm cuts show the cloth they cut in shade instead of a black oval (D579), and grime sits in fold valleys, at hems and under the arms instead of random blotches (D580). The goblin's patches and the human's darn are raised, rolled and stitched (D581); shoulder seams, collars and the elf's cape hem carry a running stitch (D582); collars gather in short folds and the goblin's shirt drapes from the armpits (D583); the skin under the clothes is no longer meshed (D584).
- Beauty, path-traced and review renders no longer print a seam of the neighbouring strip at the edge of every texture band: the renderers' trim-sheet sample stays inside the strip row or cell it was asked for at every mip (the faction plaza read as wooden planks, D600).
- Human, elf and goblin busts' far hair: the human's and the elf's LOD2 is a hair shell in the hair's colour with a few locks lying on it instead of flat planes and shards standing off a bald skull (D585); LOD1's cards hug the head and cover it, and the elf's fall hangs instead of fanning out (D586); the human's beard keeps its value at LOD2 (D587); the elf's far locks no longer lie over its ears (D588); the goblin's wisps curl on the dome instead of standing off as quills (D589).
