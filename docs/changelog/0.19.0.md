# ts23d 0.19.0

Released 2026-11-21 · [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@developerz.ai/ts23d/v/0.19.0) · [diff v0.18.1...v0.19.0](https://github.com/developerz-ai/ts23d/compare/v0.18.1...v0.19.0) · [all releases](README.md) · [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md)

Hair shows fine strands and a sheen instead of straw, skin pores hold an even scale on every triangle, far-LOD busts keep their faces, the orc wears real forged steel, and `ctx.use`, bone clips, sockets and `t3d shot --lods` export what you built.

### Fixed
- Skin pores no longer smear into streaks on large triangles or stretch on tilted ones (face busts, the human, elf, goblin and orc heads, joint_demo): the pore strips are read at an even texel density at every LOD (13 projection planes; a triangle too tall for a band cell keeps true scale on its own plane), measured by `texelSpread` (D550).
- The far LOD busts (human, elf, goblin heads) keep their eye sockets, nose and lips: LOD2 meshes the face at half the head's cell (LOD2 budgets 3 500, 3 650 and 3 300 triangles), and the eyes no longer bulge out of the lids (D551).
- LOD1 bust skin no longer blotches: its vertices filter the paint over their faces instead of point-sampling it (D552).
- Wool tunics on the face busts no longer break into triangular patches where the knit's rib changes direction: directional strips (cloth, the lids' and lips' lines) keep the level projection planes, only the pore strips take D550's thirteen (D562).
- A clip that keys a used skinned child's morph weights no longer crashes export with `T3D_PART_UNKNOWN`: a part keyed by a `weights` track keeps its own node.
- `ctx.use` with a `yaw` turns the child's bone clips with it, so a turned creature bends its joints the right way.
- A socket that rides a bone keeps its world yaw in the GLB.
- `ctx.use` keeps the child's `glow`, `gaps`, `creases` and `checks` (prefixed): a used lamp stays lit, a used cabinet keeps its declared seam, and the child's checks run in the parent.
- `t3d shot` and `build --shot` with `--lods` not starting at 0 draw the hero, thumb and sheet from LOD0 and LOD2.
- `t3d build <dir>` on a directory with no recipe fails with `T3D_RECIPE_NOT_FOUND` instead of reporting success.
- Head hair in the path-traced hero (human and elf busts, face busts) shows a soft sheen band along the strands instead of flat, straw-like ribbons: the strand tangent turns smoothly along a bent card, and smooth fibres gather their light about the highlight; fur is unchanged. The elf's hair sweeps back over the crown, without the bald-looking disc. Head-hair cards show separate fine strands from root to tip instead of solid ribbons (the `hair` and `lock` cells of the strand strips; fur cells unchanged), and the human and elf hairlines thin out into the skin with sparse baby hairs.
- `orc_head` reads as a forged, worn warrior bust at every distance: the skull has temporal planes, an occipital crest and a break at the nape; the pauldrons are bare dark steel built from lapped, riveted, hammer-peened plates with rolled edges, worn by measured weathering (rubbed edges, scratches, rust under the rivets, soot in the hollows); the far LOD keeps a face with lit eyes, clean round pauldrons and a solid beard.
