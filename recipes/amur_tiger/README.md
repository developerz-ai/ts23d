# amur_tiger — Amur (Siberian) tiger, adult male, relaxed standing

*Panthera tigris altaica* (now *P. t. tigris*, mainland lineage): the largest living cat and the
first quadruped mammal in the gallery, sculpted from code on a real skeleton. It is the test bed
for the planned `creature` kind ([roadmap M4](../../docs/plans/roadmap.md)): skeleton landmarks →
muscle masses → head → paws → fur → stripes → tone, each stage its own module.

Pose: relaxed standing, weight on all four legs, head a little below the back line, mouth just
open (canines show), tail hanging in a gentle J whose tip turns up just above the ground.
Winter coat: the long Amur cheek ruff, belly fringe, pale ground colour.

## Skeleton and stance (Panthera; borrowed from the plates below)

- **Digitigrade** on all four feet: wrist and hock clear of the ground; the hock sits at ~⅓ of
  the hind leg height (0.25 m), the carpal pad just behind the front paw.
- Fore leg: scapula lies steep on the ribcage (top = the withers, the highest point of the back),
  shoulder joint forward at the brisket's upper third, **elbow back and tucked against the chest**
  at about the brisket's level, forearm vertical and heavy (the cat's grappling arm), short
  pastern sloping forward, a broad round paw.
- Hind leg: high hip, **stifle (knee) forward** level with the belly, long tibia back to the hock,
  near-vertical metatarsus, narrower oval paw.
- Back: level withers → slight dip behind the shoulders → lumbar rise → croup sloping to the
  tail root. Loose primordial belly fold hangs between the legs.
- Head carried low in the relaxed walk/stand (Muybridge, Schönbrunn photos): the crown sits
  just below the withers, the neck short, thick and maned in winter.

## Coat and stripe logic (what `coat.ts` paints)

- **Ground colour**: Amur winter coat pale rusty-yellow/orange, warmer and darker along the spine;
  **white** underside from chin to tail root: throat, chest, belly (reaching high on the lower
  flank), inner legs, the cheeks and muzzle (Wikipedia: Tiger, "Coat").
- **Stripes** black to dark brown, narrow (Amur: narrower and more widely spaced than Bengal),
  each tiger unique, **left and right sides differ**. On the trunk they run **dorso-ventrally**
  (spine → belly), thinning and breaking up toward the white belly; some fork (Y), some pair
  (double stripes), some stop as spots. They get **denser toward the rear**. On the hind thigh
  they curve back round the haunch; on the legs and forehead they run **horizontally** (bands);
  forelegs carry few, mostly on the back and inner side.
- **Tail**: thick black **rings** (about 8–10 on the second half), a **black tip**.
- **Face mask**: white patch above each eye, black "eyeliner" running back from the outer
  corner, 3–4 black lines on each white cheek fanning back into the ruff, narrow horizontal
  forehead stripes, an orange bridge from the brow to the nose, pink nose leather rimmed black,
  a black lip line, white chin with a dark spot behind the lip, black whisker-spot rows.
- **Ears**: short, round; black backs with a **white ocellus** (eye-spot) in the middle, pale fur
  inside the pinna.
- **Eyes**: round pupil, amber/yellow iris (photos).

## Value and tone

Darker dorsal stripe and deeper orange on the back, pale belly (countershading), value rising
from the dark-stained feet to the head; the head is the focal point (highest contrast: white
mask against black stripes and amber eyes).

**Density.** The sculpted surface is the coat, not the skin. The gate fills the mesh with
`EFFECTIVE_DENSITY` = 1050 − (1050 − 100) × 0.02 m × 14.5 m⁻¹ ≈ 775 kg/m³: mammal flesh
(~1050) under a ~2 cm effective hair shell (~100 kg/m³: keratin at ~8 % packing) — the coat depth
and packing are stated assumptions (winter hair 40–110 mm, Geptner & Sludskij); the surface ÷
volume ratio is measured and held by a test. At plain 1000 kg/m³ the furred volume weighs ~300 kg.

**D22.** The body is one sculpt, so it is never double-counted (the voxel union and the gate's
body volume agree to 0.1 %). The head, jaw, tail and ear roots overlap the body or the head by
design (grown roots), and the gate still sums those overlaps — a few kg, not measured.

**Allometry** uses a stated bone fraction (humerus/femur ≈ 0.2 of the furred upper-limb width);
it is not sourced, so the test only guards against gross errors (deer legs on a tiger).

## Status / known gaps

- No fur cards or shells (planned in M4): the coat is sculpted clumps + relief + vertex paint on
  the `plain` strip (the library `fur` strip's brown albedo mutes the orange).
- Whiskers are not modelled as strands; the whisker pads carry the dark spot rows.
- Stripe edges stair-step at the 1.2 cm body cell (vertex colour); LOD2 stripes are blotchy.
- No wear yet (muddy paws, scars, damp fur): critique `wear` 4.
- Eye sockets are checked by ray probes in the test (enclosure, open pupil, lid cover, gaze), not
  a `socket` joint: the joints gate compares the head's whole section with the 3 cm eye.
- Anatomical folds (mouth, nostrils, eyelids, gape, armpits, carpus, hock, groin, flank fold,
  toe partings) are declared `creases` with reasons so QA `connections` skips them.
- Critique (`out/amur_tiger/critique.json`): overall 6 — silhouette 7, proportions 7, materials
  5, detail 6, wear 4, LOD 7, technical 8.
