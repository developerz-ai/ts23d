# Jupiter — Central Pacific No. 60, 4-4-0 "American" (Schenectady 1868)

## Identity
The 1979 operating replica at Golden Spike National Historical Park, in its blue / red / gold
Golden Spike livery. Signature features: balloon stack, oil headlamp on a bracket, brass bell and
dome, cowcatcher pilot, wooden cab with arched windows, 4-wheel lead truck, two coupled drivers
with side rods, an 8-wheel tender with JUPITER lettering. The recipe is a family over the Whyte
arrangement: frame, boiler and rods are laid out from `'4-4-0'`.
