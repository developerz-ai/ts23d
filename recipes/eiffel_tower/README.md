# Eiffel Tower — as of 2022 (330 m with the DAB antenna), 1889 structure

## Identity
Variant: the tower as it stands since 15 March 2022 (DAB antenna, 330 m to the tip) on the 1889
wrought-iron structure; 1st/2nd-floor superstructures simplified to their railings and glazed
pavilions. Chosen because 330 m is the number every visitor knows and the lattice is unchanged.

Signature features (what makes it THIS object):
1. Four separate curved legs ("piles"), each a square lattice column of four box-section
   arbalétriers (corner uprights), joining into one shaft above the 2nd floor.
2. The concave skyline profile — 29 panels per face, steep at the base (65.8° face slope), vertical
   at the top — the exponential-looking curve.
3. The four decorative semicircular arches between the legs under the 1st floor (Sauvestre).
4. The deep lattice belt girder of the 1st floor (with the frieze of 72 names) and the lighter 2nd.
5. The small 3rd-floor cabin, the campanile of four arches with the lantern, and the antenna mast.
6. Three graduated shades of "Eiffel Tower Brown", darkest at the bottom.

### Structure (plate PL. I "Diagrammes horizontaux")
- Panels 1–11 (z 0 → 109.1): four separate legs, each a square of 4 arbalétriers (a inner, b/d sides, c outer corner).
- Panels 12–17 (z 109.1 → 175.6): the inner arbalétriers (a) stop; 8 uprights on the outer ring, (c) at corners, (b)/(d) about 30% in from each corner.
- Panels 18–29 (z 175.6 → 269.8): 4 corner uprights.
- Every face of every panel: a horizontal and a St Andrew's cross; each member is itself a lattice girder (two flanges + zig-zag web): https://www.gracesguide.co.uk/Eiffel_Tower ("box section", angle-iron corners).

### Colour
Three graduated shades of "Eiffel Tower Brown", darkest at the bottom, lightest at the top, so it
reads as one colour against the sky: https://www.nationalgeographic.com/travel/article/behind-the-scenes-eiffel-tower-paris ·
https://csmonitor.com/2001/1129/p7s1-woeu.html. Since the 20th campaign (2019–2024) it is back to
Eiffel's 1907 yellow-brown: https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/the-monument/painting-eiffel-tower.
No hex/RAL published; chosen by eye from photos: bottom `#5e4834`, middle `#715a40`, top `#87704f`.

1–11), `arch_0..3` (between legs; 0 +X, 1 +Y, 2 −X, 3 −Y), `belt_1`, `platform_1`, `railing_1`,

## Open questions
- **Profile scale.** Table 1 gives a ground half width of 53.46 m, but the official footprint is
  124.90 m (half 62.45). The table's top (w = 5 → 10 m) matches the plate, so the table is most
  likely measured on the arbalétrier axes / inside face lines. Chosen: the table shape scaled by
  62.45 / 53.459 = 1.168 so the outer faces hit 124.90 m at the ground. Cross-checks: 1st-floor
  outer side 70.69 m overhangs the scaled legs (67.4 m) slightly, 2nd floor (40.96 m) overhangs the
  shaft (34.9 m), as in the plate.
- **Leg plan width above the ground.** Only the ground value (25.34 m) is published. Chosen: linear
  taper to 0.6 × the half width at panel 11 (z = 109.1), matching the plate's Fig. 4 where (b)/(d)
  sit ~30% in from the corners.
- **Arbalétrier section.** Not published in the sources found; chosen 1.4 m at the ground tapering to
  0.7 m at the top (by eye from the plate at 1:1000).
- **Arch.** Circle through the inner leg feet (74.24 m) and the keystone (38.90 m): R = 37.16 m,
  centre 1.74 m above ground.
- **Floor areas** disagree (official ft² vs fr.wikipedia m²); not used.
